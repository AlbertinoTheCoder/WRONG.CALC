## Your math, your problem.

WRONG.CALC brings an unnecessarily premium calculator experience to your browser, complete with terrible answers, rebellious buttons, emotional instability, and mathematical disasters.

### Features

- **Confidently incorrect** — Wrong answers delivered with absolute confidence.
- **Runaway equals** — The equals button grows legs and escapes for 10 seconds.
- **Calculator personalities** — Angry, smug, confused, sad, and more.
- **Button union** — Your keypad goes on strike.
- **Math autocorrect** — Your numbers change without permission.
- **Premium subtraction** — Unlock subtraction through a completely fake purchase.
- **Fake AI support** — Customer service makes everything worse.
- **Calculator court** — Get sued for mathematical misconduct.
- **Haunted calculator** — Ghost numbers invade your display.
- **Shapeshifting** — The calculator transforms into a microwave or TV remote.
- **Number auctions** — Numbers bid to become your answer.
- **Conspiracy board** — Discover the secrets of Big Algebra.
- **Chaos Lab** — Manually trigger mathematical disasters.

## Chaos Lab

An interactive disaster control panel where you can unleash chaos whenever you want.

Trigger fake system crashes, kidnap numbers, summon rival calculators, start auctions, activate fake antivirus scans, and send the equals button running for its life.

All effects happen locally inside your browser.

## Run locally

Clone the repository:

```bash
git clone https://github.com/AlbertinoTheCoder/WRONG.CALC.git
```

Then open `index.html` in your browser.

No installations, accounts, backend, or API keys required.

## Built with

HTML5 · CSS3 · Vanilla JavaScript

No frameworks. No external math libraries. Absolutely no intelligence.

## About

WRONG.CALC is a parody calculator created purely for entertainment.

Its mathematical answers are deliberately unreliable. Do not use it for homework, taxes, engineering, or anything requiring accurate calculations.

All purchases, downloads, crashes, and security warnings are simulated.
